## Guided setup: a first-run walkthrough

Today each setup piece lives in its own tab. The next build walks a new user through all of them in order, one step
per screen, with progress dots and a skip option:

1. **Add your clothes.** Photos, product links or receipts, into the closet.
2. **Upload your face.** A face close-up, for color analysis (color season, best colors, metals).
3. **Upload a full-body photo.** Used for photo try-on and size estimates, with the consent box.
4. **Tell us your style.** Style words, comfort rules, budget and house rules.
5. **Inspiration.** Upload inspo photos or Pinterest boards, or pick from style choices.
6. **Choose your style archetypes.** (Partly built 2026-10-26: archetype tiles, quiz and blend in Margaux-Sloan's card.)
   The guided version shows the 7 archetypes (Classic, Elegant Chic, Natural, Romantic, Dramatic, Creative, Gamine)
   as mood boards; the user picks a foundation, a current-season archetype and an accent. Sources: Emma Irene
   Cavanagh's style archetypes, Capitol Hill Clothiers' style guide, Flourish Styling's archetypes.

One stylist throughout: Margaux-Sloan, who asks follow-up questions and learns the user's point of view (built
2026-10-26), not several stylist personas.

## From setup to transformation

7. **Recommend toward the target look.** From the closet and the chosen aesthetics, recommend clothes, looks and pieces
   that move the user step by step from what they wear now toward the look they want, a few pieces at a time
   (building on the Wardrobe Recommender's "next 3 pieces" and its mock-ups).
8. **Over time, a unique look.** As feedback, wears, bangers and returns build up, the stylist blends the chosen
   aesthetics with what the user actually loves and develops a signature look of their own, not a copy of one
   aesthetic.
