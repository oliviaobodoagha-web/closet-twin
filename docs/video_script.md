## 1 · The problem (0:00–0:20)

**Click:** nothing yet. **Today** tab on Sample.

- This is Closet Twin, my AI stylist, Margaux-Sloan. She dresses me from clothes I already own, for the day I actually have.

## 5 · Shopping in 12 W (1:35–2:00)

**Click:** **Shop** tab → type **black leather loafers** → search.
**On screen:** her answer right away: she didn't search because I said no to that (no charge).

- If a request breaks one of my no's, she says so right away, for free.
- Otherwise the Scout searches real stores, a link is kept only if the search actually visited that page, the Fit Tailor checks whether "12 W" is really listed, and the Critic gives a second opinion.
- A bug I found and fixed: at first it threw away good finds, so 4 of 11 searches came back empty. Now it trusts any page the search really opened.
- My real test: 3 searches found a Naturalizer flat and three Mary Janes in 12 W that I'd buy.

Leave **Search real stores in my size** unticked (a live search costs money).

## 6 · What it costs (2:00–2:15)

**Click:** switch to the slides tab, **slide 7**.
**On screen:** the cost numbers and the text vs. pictures bar.

- Planning a whole day is about half a cent ($0.0050); one search of real stores is $0.055.
- About $2.61 per person per month. Pictures are 85% of that, mostly the daily try-on photo. All the text is about $0.40.
- Making the try-on optional drops it to $0.84 a month. Speeding everything up hurt: the Critic contradicted itself, so the Planner and Critic now think longer. Still half a cent a plan.

## 7 · Safety (2:15–2:30)

**Click:** **slide 8**.
**On screen:** the safety list and "63/63".

- A review or web page that says "ignore your rules" is treated as plain text, never as an order.
- The shopping helper never sees my measurements, and photos only go out with my OK.
- A piece I don't own or a link nobody visited is rejected, and try-ons keep my face and body as they are, no slimming.
- 63 of 63 safety tests pass.

## 8 · Impact (2:30–2:48)

**Click:** **slide 9**.
**On screen:** 105 → 5.5 min, a week → 10 min, the plan I approved.

- I tested it on my real closet and calendar: a busy recruiting-day outfit went from 105 minutes to 5.5. About 19× faster, and I loved the look.
- Shoes in 12 wide I'd actually buy: from about a week to 10 minutes, 3 searches for $0.18.
- At 10 minutes saved a morning, that's about 60 hours a year per person. Before, I had to pick outfits to match my shoes instead of the other way around.
